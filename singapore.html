Explore Singapore

"A journey into the Lion City—where futuristic innovation meets a rich cultural tapestry."

About Singapore

Once a colonial trading post, Singapore is now a thriving global financial hub and one of the world's most prosperous nations. Known as the Lion City, it is celebrated for its pristine streets, incredible safety, and a beautiful multicultural harmony of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Eurasian communities.

From the futuristic supertrees of Gardens by the Bay to the vibrant, historic shophouses of Chinatown and Little India, Singapore is a masterclass in urban planning. It is a city-state where nature and architecture blend seamlessly, living up to its reputation as a true "City in a Garden."

Why Visit Singapore?

A City in a Garden

Experience the perfect harmony of lush green spaces and cutting-edge urban design, from the iconic Gardens by the Bay to the UNESCO-listed Botanic Gardens.

Hawker Culture & Fine Dining

Indulge in a melting pot of flavors. Savor world-class Michelin-starred cuisine or enjoy affordable, mouth-watering local dishes at historic hawker centers.

Unmatched Safety

Enjoy peace of mind in one of the safest, cleanest, and most efficiently run cities in the world, boasting a world-class public transit system.

What to Do in Singapore

Discover Marina Bay Sands

Marvel at the iconic ship-like architecture, visit the SkyPark observation deck, and enjoy breathtaking views of the city skyline.

Wander Chinatown & Little India

Immerse yourself in rich heritage, vibrant colors, and aromatic spices in Singapore's historic cultural enclaves.

Visit the ArtScience Museum

Uncover the fascinating intersection of art, science, and technology through immersive, cutting-edge exhibits.

Gardens by the Bay

Walk beneath the awe-inspiring Supertrees and explore the massive, climate-controlled Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories.

Singapore Botanic Gardens

Stroll through the lush, sprawling grounds of this UNESCO World Heritage site and admire the magnificent National Orchid Garden.

Explore the Night Safari

Embark on an unforgettable nocturnal adventure at the world's first night zoo, observing fascinating wildlife in naturalistic habitats.

Thrills at Sentosa Island

Escape to Singapore's premier island resort destination, featuring pristine beaches, Universal Studios, and luxury beach clubs.

Marvel at Jewel Changi

Experience the magic of the HSBC Rain Vortex, the world's tallest indoor waterfall, surrounded by a lush indoor forest.

Ride the Singapore Flyer

Take a scenic flight on Asia's largest giant observation wheel for panoramic views stretching as far as neighboring Malaysia.
